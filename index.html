Prodigy Fitness

A training and nutrition app for families and their coaches: workout plans and logging, a training calendar, food diary and meal plans, body progress, and printing of your workout and diet plan.

Android: get Prodigy Fitness on Google Play.

iPhone, iPad or computer: use the web app with the same account. On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen and it opens like an app.
